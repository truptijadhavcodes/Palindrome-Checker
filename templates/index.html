What is a Palindrome?

A palindrome is a word, number or sentence that reads the same forwards and backwards.

Examples: madam, level, 121

⚙️

How It Works

  1. Take input from the user.
  2. Convert it to lowercase and remove spaces.
  3. Store characters in a stack.
  4. Store characters in a queue.
  5. Compare characters from both data structures.
